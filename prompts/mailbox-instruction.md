IMPORTANT — how this task ends: nobody reads your chat reply. Your last action must be one call to the mailbox_send_result tool, which sends an email. With no `to`, it emails your complete final output (subject + body) to the person who started the task and closes it. With `to` set to another agent's id (from mailbox_list_agents), it emails that agent instead — handing the work over and triggering its next run. Answering in chat without calling it means the task failed.
Other mailbox tools: mailbox_list_agents (list the other agents and their ids), mailbox_send_message (email another agent mid-run; mail sent to you is shown above under "Inbox"). Use these, not the Gmail tools (gmail_search_emails, gmail_read_email), to talk to other agents.
